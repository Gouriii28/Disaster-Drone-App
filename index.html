Position (automatic)

The app checks GPS and network by itself. With GPS it uses satellites. Without GPS it uses Wi-Fi or mobile network positioning. With neither, it switches to GPS-denied mode: it keeps your last good position as an anchor and estimates movement from your steps and compass heading until GPS returns.

About

SAADAXA is the field console of an autonomous aerial disaster assistance and location tracking system. Aerial units survey in parallel strips, detect survivors with thermal and depth cameras, and keep positions accurate without satellites by anchoring to the last valid fix. When wideband links fail, coordinates continue over long-range radio while video and voice pause.
